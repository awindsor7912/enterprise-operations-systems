
# BOMS — Conceptual Systems Design

## Purpose

This document describes general design considerations relevant to business systems supporting operational workflows, process automation, and reporting.

It does not document the architecture of Infratech Corporation's internal application.

---

## Functional Design Areas

### Workflow Management

Structured approaches to tracking tasks, processing responsibilities, and work progression.

### Business Process Automation

Reducing repetitive administrative activities through appropriate automation and standardization.

### Data Quality

Using validation and review processes to improve consistency and identify incomplete or conflicting information.

### Operational Visibility

Presenting relevant information to users according to their responsibilities and business needs.

### Reporting & Analysis

Summarizing operational activity to support performance evaluation and informed decision-making.

### Maintainability

Documenting functional requirements, considering changing business needs, and evaluating opportunities for iterative improvement.

---

## Design Considerations

Common considerations for enterprise workflow applications include:

- Consistent processing behavior.
- Usability and accessibility.
- Appropriate information visibility.
- Reliability and error handling.
- Scalability.
- Data accuracy.
- Supportability.
- Operational continuity.

These are generalized design principles, not claims about specific controls or technologies implemented by the employer.
