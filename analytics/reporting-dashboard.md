# Reporting and operational intelligence

## Phase I

Power BI was used to support operational reporting and visibility into billing workflow activity.

Public portfolio discussion is limited to general dashboard objectives, such as workload oversight, processing status, and exception awareness.

## Phase II

The limited-user pilot extends reporting toward role-appropriate views and broader operational trends.

Described areas include production activity, billing adjustments, equipment-related measures, and revenue-oriented analysis.

## Measurement integrity

This repository includes **no live datasets, real dashboards or company metrics**.

Illustrative reporting concepts should not be mistaken for verified operational performance.

Financial formulas, semantic models, exact measures, and company reporting definitions are withheld.
