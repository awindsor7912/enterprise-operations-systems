
# BOMS — Operational Reporting & Analytics

## Overview

Operational reporting supports the evaluation of business activity, workflow performance, processing consistency, and opportunities for improvement.

The BOMS initiatives demonstrate the application of reporting and analytical concepts to complex administrative processes.

This document presents generalized reporting objectives rather than actual employer dashboards, datasets, or performance measures.

---

## Phase I — Workflow Visibility

Phase I emphasized improving visibility into structured administrative workflows.

General reporting objectives included:

- Workload monitoring.
- Processing status visibility.
- Identification of outstanding items.
- Exception awareness.
- Operational coordination.

---

## Phase II — Integrated Operational Intelligence

Phase II expands reporting considerations toward more personalized and comprehensive operational information.

General functional categories include:

### Role-Relevant Reporting

Presenting information appropriate to individual responsibilities and decision-making needs.

### Workflow Performance

Supporting analysis of activity, processing trends, and operational bottlenecks.

### Data Quality & Validation

Improving visibility into incomplete, inconsistent, or potentially incorrect information.

### Operational Trends

Providing comparative information that supports management evaluation and process improvement.

### Exception Monitoring

Helping users identify and prioritize items requiring additional attention.

---

## Reporting Design Principles

Effective operational reporting should consider:

- Clear metric definitions.
- Consistent interpretation.
- Appropriate data validation.
- Relevant reporting periods.
- User-specific information needs.
- Understandable visual presentation.
- Accurate distinction between actual results and estimates.

---

## Measurement Integrity

No actual employer datasets, financial figures, confidential reporting definitions, or production dashboards are included in this repository.

Any future illustrative examples should use independently created, fictional information and should not reproduce proprietary business logic.

Reported improvements should be substantiated before being presented as quantified outcomes.
