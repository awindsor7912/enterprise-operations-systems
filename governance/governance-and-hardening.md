# Data governance and system reliability — general considerations

A billing-related automation initiative should assess:

- **Access and least privilege:** Users should access only appropriate records and functions.
- **Data quality:** Validation should detect missing or inconsistent information.
- **Error handling:** Exceptions should remain observable and reviewable.
- **Change management:** Functional changes require appropriate review and testing.
- **Business continuity:** Workflow maintenance should not depend on undocumented individual knowledge.
- **Record retention:** Actual retention must follow authorized organizational policy.

These are **design considerations**, not assertions that particular security controls, audit facilities or compliance certifications have been implemented.

Internal security configuration and operational procedures are not public.
